# Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Every employee must enrol in MFA using the Authenticator app before accessing email, VPN or SaaS tools.

## New phone
If you changed phones, sign in to the Self-Service portal from a trusted laptop and choose "Re-register MFA device". If you no longer have access to any trusted device, raise a ticket; the Service Desk will verify your identity by video call before resetting MFA.

## Not receiving push notifications
Check that notifications are enabled for the Authenticator app and that your phone time is set automatically. As a fallback, use the 6-digit code shown in the app.
