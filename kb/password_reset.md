# Password reset and account lockout

## Resetting a forgotten password
Go to the password portal at passwords.example-corp.com and choose "Forgot password". You need your registered phone for the MFA code. New passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of the last 10 passwords.

## Account locked
Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes. If you need access sooner, raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk and include your employee ID. The Service Desk will never ask for your password.

## Password expiry
Passwords expire every 90 days. You get email reminders 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.
