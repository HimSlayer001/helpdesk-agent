# Access to applications (Slack, Jira, Salesforce, GitHub)

Access to business applications is role-based and approved by your manager.

## Requesting access
Raise an access request ticket naming the application, the role or group you need, and your manager. Access is usually granted within 1 business day after manager approval.

## GitHub organisation
Engineers need a GitHub account with MFA enabled and their corporate email verified before being added to the organisation.

## Removing access
Managers request access removal for leavers; it is completed on the employee's last working day.
