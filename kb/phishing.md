# Reporting phishing and security incidents

## Suspicious email
Do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook, which sends the email to the Security Operations team and removes it from your inbox.

## If you clicked a link or entered your password
Change your password immediately from the password portal, then raise a security incident ticket marked high priority. Disconnect from the VPN until Security contacts you.

## Lost or stolen device
Report it within 1 hour by raising a high-priority security incident ticket. Security will remotely lock and wipe the device.
