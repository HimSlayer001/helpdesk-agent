# Laptop setup and hardware

## New laptop
New hires receive a pre-configured laptop. On first boot, sign in with SSO; device management will install standard apps within about 30 minutes. Keep the laptop on the charger and connected to the internet during this time.

## Hardware faults
For a broken screen, keyboard or battery issue, raise a hardware ticket with the asset tag on the bottom of the laptop. A loaner laptop is available from the IT desk on floor 3 in the Bangalore office.

## Disk full
Empty the Downloads folder and Trash first. Do not delete anything under /Library or C:\Windows. Request extra storage through a ticket if the problem continues.
