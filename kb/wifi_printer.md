# Office Wi-Fi and printing

## Wi-Fi
Employees connect to the CORP-SECURE network using SSO credentials. Guests use CORP-GUEST with the daily password shown at reception. Personal devices must not join CORP-SECURE.

## Printing
Printers use secure release: send the job to the "FollowMe" queue, then tap your badge on any printer to release it. Jobs are deleted after 24 hours if not printed.
